I've been saving quotes to a notes app for over a decade. Mostly from books, sometimes from a song or a friend. It seemed time to give them a pretty home.

Use the arrow keys to navigate, or swipe if you're on your phone, which you probably are. The quote number lives in the URL so you can share individual ones if you'd like.

Made by Monica Dinculescu, a big reader with some friends.
